# Example business travel policy

This document is fictional demonstration content for evaluating the prototype. It is not an organisational policy.

## Booking and expenses

Employees should book business travel at least 14 calendar days before departure when practical. A manager must approve travel before any booking is made. Itemised receipts are required for expenses greater than €25.

## Mileage

Approved use of a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at €0.35 per kilometre. The employee must record the journey date, business purpose, and distance claimed.
