# Example information security policy

## Reporting a suspected incident

Employees must report suspected security incidents to the Security Operations team within 24 hours of discovery. Include the time observed, affected system, and a short description. Do not include passwords in the report.

## Password requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access. Passwords must not be shared with colleagues or stored in plain text.

## Data retention

Support tickets are retained for 180 days after closure. Security incident records are retained for 7 years. Legal holds suspend normal deletion schedules.
