# Example records retention policy

This document is fictional demonstration content for evaluating the prototype. It is not an organisational policy.

## Retention periods

Support tickets are retained for 180 days after closure. Security incident records are retained for 7 years. Recruitment records for unsuccessful applicants are retained for 12 months after the hiring decision.

## Exceptions

Legal holds suspend normal deletion schedules. The Records Management team reviews retention schedules every January.
