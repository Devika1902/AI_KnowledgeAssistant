# Example IT support service levels

This document is fictional demonstration content for evaluating the prototype. It is not an organisational policy.

## Support response

Critical incidents receive an initial response within 1 business hour during support hours. Standard service requests receive an initial response within 2 business days. These are response targets, not guaranteed resolution times.

## Managed devices

Managed devices must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Critical security updates must be installed within 14 calendar days of release.
