# Example leave and attendance policy

This document is fictional demonstration content for evaluating the prototype. It is not an organisational policy.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, excluding public holidays. Leave requests should be submitted at least 10 calendar days before the first day requested. A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried over, and those days must be used by 31 March.

## Sickness absence

Employees who cannot work because of illness should notify their manager by 9:00 a.m. local time on the first day of absence.
